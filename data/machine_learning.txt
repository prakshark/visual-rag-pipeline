What is Machine Learning?Instead of writing step-by-step rules, developers feed data into algorithms to find hidden patterns. Traditional programming uses rules and data to produce answers, but machine learning uses data and answers to discover the rules. As the computer processes more examples, its internal logic adjusts, making its predictions and decisions more accurate over time.Types of LearningSystems learn in three main ways: supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. Supervised learning uses labeled data with correct answers, like teaching an algorithm to spot spam emails. Unsupervised learning explores unlabeled data to find hidden groupings or structures on its own. Reinforcement learning relies on trial and error, using rewards and penalties to guide optimal actions.Real-World ImpactMachine learning powers many everyday tools, such as streaming recommendations, voice assistants, and fraud detection systems. It handles complex tasks that are too intricate for manual coding. By turning massive datasets into smart automation, this technology continues to transform industries worldwide.
